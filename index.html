The phases

How a project moves.

Every project runs the same sequence. What changes is how long each phase takes and where the risk sits.

1

Feasibility

Zoning and setback review, utility and topography checks, a hard-cost estimate, and a comp-driven view of exit value. If the spread isn’t there, this is where we say so.

2

Acquisition & structuring

Contract, diligence period, entity formation, and the construction loan. We structure the deal and the debt before we spend money on drawings.

3

Pre-construction

Design and plan review, permit submittal, trade bids, and a locked budget and schedule. Demolition of the existing structure happens at the end of this phase.

4

Construction

Trade coordination, draw management, inspections, and selections. You get regular updates against the same schedule and budget we’re holding the trades to.

5

Delivery & sale

Punch list, certificate of occupancy, and handover — whether the house goes to a buyer who commissioned it or to the open market.
